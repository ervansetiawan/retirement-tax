How Tax-Minimization Protects Your Wealth

1. Standard Deduction Harvesting (0% Tax)

Every year, the standard deduction ($14,600 Single, $29,200 MFJ, plus $1,550-$1,950 extra for age 65+) provides 0% tax headroom. We withdraw pre-tax funds into this space 100% tax-free.

2. Strategic Bracket-Filling (10% & 12%)

Draining taxable accounts first allows pre-tax accounts to balloon into a "tax bomb" triggering heavy RMD taxes at ages 73-75. Filling lower brackets early prevents future tax spikes.

3. Roth IRA as a High-Tax Shield

When expenses exceed low ordinary brackets, Roth withdrawals cover the rest tax-free, preventing you from crossing into 22%/24%+ brackets or Medicare IRMAA penalties.

4. SECURE 2.0 RMD Compliance

Mandatory Required Minimum Distributions start at age 73 (or 75 for birth year 1960+), automatically calculated and satisfied from pre-tax buckets.

5. Pre-59½ Early Penalty Avoidance (IRC § 72(t))

Withdrawing pre-tax funds before age 59½ incurs a 10% IRS penalty. Our Smart Strategy shields you by tapping Brokerage and Roth principal first, reserving pre-tax accounts until age 60 (or toggling the Rule of 55 / SEPP exemption).

6. Preferential Long-Term Capital Gains (0% & 15% LTCG)

Brokerage withdrawals are split into tax-free principal (adjusted via the Cost Basis slider) and taxable capital gains. Gains stack on top of ordinary income, utilizing the 0% LTCG threshold ($94,050 MFJ / $47,025 Single in 2024, inflation-indexed) to achieve zero capital gains tax.

7. Roth Conversions & Defusing Future RMDs

Converting pre-tax balances to Roth in early retirement locks in lower ordinary brackets (10%, 12%, or 22%) and permanently eliminates Age 73/75 RMD tax spikes. Converted funds grow tax-free forever, and conversions carry 0% early withdrawal penalty.

8. Medicare IRMAA Surcharges & 2-Year Lookback Rule

At age 65, Medicare Part B & D premiums apply income-based surcharges (IRMAA) with steep cliffs. Under statutory rules, your surcharge is determined by your MAGI from 2 years prior. Strategic Roth conversions and tax-free Roth withdrawals shield you from crossing IRMAA cliff thresholds.

9. Minnesota & State Tax Optimization

Minnesota taxes retirement distributions and taxable capital gains progressively across 5.35% to 9.85% brackets (with a bonus senior standard deduction at age 65+). Our Smart Strategy coordinates withdrawals so you smooth state brackets alongside federal brackets, while displaying exact lifetime comparisons to Minnesota state taxes.
